About us

About the SBHS Aerospace Club

We're a student-run engineering club at South Brunswick High School in Monmouth Junction, New Jersey.

Who we are

The SBHS Aerospace Club is where students build real aerospace projects: rockets, flight electronics, CAD models, code, and science payloads. The SBHS club is new this fall. It grew out of South Brunswick Aerospace Club, a nonprofit that students in South Brunswick started in 2023, which still runs outreach events like community rocket launches.

You can be involved in two ways. General members come to monthly meetings, pick up skills like circuit design, data analysis, and fabrication, and work on challenges during meetings. Competitive members do all of that and also join a team for a specific competition, which means a lot of work outside of meetings.

Before the school club existed, our members competed as the nonprofit. Those teams made the American Rocketry Challenge National Finals in 2024, 2025, and 2026, and in 2026 our NASA TechRise team was named a Student Challenge winner.

How people learn here

Most members join with no engineering background, and that's normal.

At meetings

Members who already know CAD, simulation, circuits, or code teach it to everyone else at general meetings.

On a team

New competitive members work right next to the people who built our earlier rockets and payloads.

From past projects

Our CAD files and code live on GitHub, so nobody has to start from scratch.

Who runs things

A student executive board runs the club, with help from our club advisor, Ms. Chishti. Meet the board.

How we got here

2023

The nonprofit starts

Students in South Brunswick started South Brunswick Aerospace Club, a nonprofit, to design and build rockets together.

2024

ARC National Finals

A team from the nonprofit qualified for the American Rocketry Challenge National Finals in The Plains, Virginia.

2025

Back at the finals

Another of our teams made it to the National Finals.

2026

TechRise and a third trip to Nationals

Our NDVI payload was picked as a TechRise Student Challenge winner, and a second ARC team made the National Finals.

Fall 2026

SBHS Aerospace Club begins

South Brunswick High School approved the Aerospace Club as a new school club, open to every SBHS student. The nonprofit keeps running outreach, like the World Space Week community launch in October 2026.
