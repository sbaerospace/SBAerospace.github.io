About the SBHS Aerospace Club

A student-run engineering club at South Brunswick High School in Monmouth Junction, New Jersey.

What the club does

The South Brunswick High School Aerospace Club is where students work on real aerospace projects: rockets, flight electronics, CAD models, code, and science experiments. Students started the club in 2023, and in fall 2026 the school approved it as an official SBHS club.

There are two ways to take part. General members come to monthly meetings, learn skills such as circuit design, data analysis, and fabrication, and work on engineering challenges. Competitive members do the same and also join a team for a specific competition, which takes significant time outside meetings.

Club teams have entered the American Rocketry Challenge each season since 2024 and reached the National Finals in 2024 and 2025. In 2026, our NASA TechRise team was named a Student Challenge winner.

How members learn

No previous engineering experience is needed. Members learn from each other.

Workshops at meetings

Experienced members teach skills at general meetings, from CAD and simulation to circuits and code.

Learning on a team

New competitive members work next to students who built earlier rockets and payloads.

Shared project files

CAD files and code from past projects are kept on the club’s GitHub, so new members can build on them.

Student leaders and an advisor

A student executive board runs the club with support from a club advisor. See the leadership team.
