About the SBHS Aerospace Club

We're a student-run engineering club at South Brunswick High School in Monmouth Junction, New Jersey.

Who we are

The SBHS Aerospace Club is where students build real aerospace projects: rockets, flight electronics, CAD models, code, and science payloads. Students started the club in 2023, and this fall the school made it an official SBHS club.

You can be involved in two ways. General members come to monthly meetings, pick up skills like circuit design, data analysis, and fabrication, and work on challenges during meetings. Competitive members do all of that and also join a team for a specific competition, which means a lot of work outside of meetings.

Club teams have entered the American Rocketry Challenge every season since 2024 and made the National Finals in 2024 and 2025. In 2026, our NASA TechRise team was named a Student Challenge winner.

How people learn here

Most members join with no engineering background, and that's normal.

At meetings

Members who already know CAD, simulation, circuits, or code teach it to everyone else at general meetings.

On a team

New competitive members work right next to the people who built our earlier rockets and payloads.

From past projects

Our CAD files and code live on GitHub, so nobody has to start from scratch.

Who runs things

A student executive board runs the club, with help from a club advisor. Meet the board.
