General and competitive members

General members

  • Attend monthly club meetings
  • Join after-school Aerospace Club events
  • Learn things like data analysis, fabrication, circuit design, and how to work through a design
  • Work on engineering challenges during meetings

Open now. No tryout, and no experience needed.

Competitive members

  • Everything general members do
  • Compete on one of our teams
  • Put a good amount of time into their project outside meetings

Picked through tryouts in the summer and October. This year's tryouts are over. How tryouts work

Technical skill areas

We use these nine skill areas to place people on competition teams. The numbers line up with the team table below and with the tryout assignments.

1.CAD

Model parts in CAD software and 3D print them.

2.Simulation

Use tools like OpenRocket and CFD to test designs before building them.

3.Computer Science

Write code for electronics and data analysis, mostly in C/C++ and Python.

4.Electronics

Design and build circuits with microcontrollers and sensors.

5.Mechanical

Put prototypes together with your hands.

6.Physics

Use physics, especially aerodynamics and forces.

7.Biology

Use biology to design experiments.

8.Chemistry

Use chemistry to design experiments.

9.Research

Read published studies and design your own experiments.
