Sponsor the SBHS Aerospace Club

Each season, South Brunswick High School Aerospace Club students need rocket parts, electronics, and money for competition fees and travel. Sponsors and in-kind donations help cover those costs.

What support pays for

Rocket materials

Body tubes, motors, parachutes, 3D-printer filament, and other parts that get used up in building and test flights.

Electronics

Microcontrollers, sensors, servos, and batteries for airbrake controllers and science payloads.

Tools

Soldering equipment, hand tools, and fabrication supplies used at meetings and team builds.

Registration and travel

Competition entry fees and travel to events such as the American Rocketry Challenge National Finals in Virginia.

How to help

We welcome financial sponsorship and in-kind donations such as parts, tools, filament, or software.

To talk about supporting the club, email us with your name or organization and what you would like to contribute.
