Team and Competitions

How the SBHS Aerospace Club is organized: student leaders, general and competitive members, the skills we teach, and the teams competing in 2026–27.

Two ways to be a member

General membership is open to all SBHS students. Competitive teams have a separate selection process.

General members

  • Attend monthly club meetings
  • Join after-school Aerospace Club events
  • Build skills such as data analysis, fabrication, circuit design, the design process, and teamwork
  • Take part in engineering challenges at meetings
  • Can earn the club on their transcript if requirements are met

No tryout and no previous experience required.

Competitive members

  • Everything general members do
  • Try out for specific teams in the summer or in October
  • Compete with their assigned team
  • Have the chance to earn awards

Competitive members are expected to put a large amount of time into their projects outside meetings.

Technical skill areas

The club uses nine skill areas to match members to competition teams. The numbers match the team table below and the tryout assignments.

1.CAD

Use computer-aided design software to create models and 3D-print parts.

2.Simulation

Use simulation tools such as OpenRocket and CFD.

3.Computer Science

Write code for electronics and data analysis, mostly in C/C++ and Python.

4.Electronics

Design and build circuits with microcontrollers and sensors.

5.Mechanical

Physically build and assemble prototypes.

6.Physics

Apply physics, especially aerodynamics and forces.

7.Biology

Apply biology to experiment design.

8.Chemistry

Apply chemistry to experiment design.

9.Research

Design experiments and use published work to conduct original research.
