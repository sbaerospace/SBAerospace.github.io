Tryout Assignments

Complete ONLY 1 Assignment · Due Oct 10 @ 11:59 PM

Click any assignment below to view its goals, parameters, and submission requirements:

1. CAD: Fin Can Design
+

Goal: Use any CAD software (e.g., Onshape) to design a fin can insert for the bottom of a model rocket. Extra engineering details are encouraged.

Parameters

  • Must fit into a standard BT-80 body tube.
  • Must be realistic to 3D print.

Submission

  • CAD file or public link (e.g. Onshape).
  • Typed paragraph (≤250 words) detailing fin count, shape, dimensions, and reasoning.
2. Simulation: OpenRocket
+

Goal: Use OpenRocket to design a competition rocket scoring <10 points compliant with 2026 American Rocketry Challenge (ARC) rules.

Parameters

  • Apogee: 750 ft | Flight duration: 36-39 sec
  • Payload: 1 raw egg (55g) | Gross weight: <650g
  • Diameter: BT-80 body tube | Length: >650 mm
  • Motor: ARC approved | Recovery: Parachute & shock cord

Submission

  • .ork simulation file.
  • Typed explanation (≤250 words) covering arrangement, stability, and challenges.
3. Computer Science: Create a Program
+

Goal: Write a program (Python, C/C++, etc.) that interacts with hardware, processes data, or solves an aerospace engineering challenge (e.g., sensor logging, trajectory math, or flight analysis).

Requirements

  • Well-commented code explaining structure.
  • Working demonstration with visuals/data charts.

Submission

  • Code files or GitHub repository.
  • Screenshots/video of program running.
  • Explanation (≤250 words) & presentation (≥5 slides).
4. Electronics: KiCad Schematic
+

Goal: Design a KiCad circuit schematic for an aerospace application (e.g., airbrakes controller, thrust vectoring, drone electronics, weather station, or avionics).

Requirements

  • Include microcontrollers, sensors, and power regulation.
  • Label all major components, nets, and connections clearly.

Submission

  • KiCad schematic file & image/screenshot.
  • Typed explanation (≤250 words) on component choices & operation.
5. Mechanical: Engineering Diagram
+

Goal: Create a scaled mechanical diagram for a competition build: ARC Rocket, MATE ROV underwater robot, Aerial Drone, or NASA Exploration Rover.

Requirements

  • Adhere to selected competition rules.
  • Clear, scaled diagram with all parts labeled.

Submission

  • Hand-drawn or CAD-drawn diagram.
  • Typed explanation (≤300 words).
6. Research Question
+

Goal: Identify a scientific research question in physics, chemistry, biology, or aerospace that you would investigate in the club (relevant to NASA TechRise, Genes in Space, or Cubes in Space).

Submission

  • Typed paper (≥300 words) with research question, motivation, literature background, and methodology.
7. Engineering Problem Proposal
+

Goal: Define an aerospace engineering challenge and propose an experiment, analytical model, or design solution.

Submission

  • Typed technical document (≥300 words) including problem statement, background research, proposed engineering solution, and relevant calculations/diagrams.
Supplemental: Resume / Portfolio (Optional)
+

Upload past technical projects, STEM coursework, or extracurriculars. (Not having past projects will NOT negatively affect your application!)
